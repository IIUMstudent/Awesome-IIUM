Note: WhatsApp groups are mostly private or invite-only. Check with your
Mahallah or Kulliyyah representatives for specific links.

## 🏛 General Categories

- **Mahallah Blocks**: Most blocks have dedicated WhatsApp groups for internal
  announcements.
- **Kulliyyah Courses**: Students often create groups for specific course
  sections at the start of each semester.
- **Societies & Clubs**: Check with club committee members for member-only
  groups.
