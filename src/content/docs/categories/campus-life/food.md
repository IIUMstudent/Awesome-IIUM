> Where students eat at every campus.

Campus food outlets are licensed and managed by the
[Residential and Services Department (RSD)](https://division.iium.edu.my/rsd/).

## 🏛 Gombak

- **Mahallah cafeterias** - Every mahallah has a cafeteria with buffet and stall
  services, and many stay open late.
- **Kulliyyah cafes** - Most kulliyyahs have a cafe nearby. Student favourites
  include **HS Cafe** (known for roti canai and nasi campur) and the
  **Engineering Cafe** (cheap breakfasts).
- **International outlets** - A few outlets on campus serve Asian, Western and
  Middle Eastern food.
- **Semester breaks and public holidays** - Outlets open on a rotation. The
  schedule is posted on the RSD website and in i-Ma'luum.

## 🌴 Kuantan, Pagoh and Gambang

The mahallat at each campus have their own cafeterias and convenience stores.
See [Campuses](../../campuses/) for what each campus offers.

## 💰 Eating on a Budget

- **Bring your own container** - Some cafeterias charge for takeaway containers.
- **Free food** - Look out for free food distribution at the mosque foyer in the
  evenings. See [Student Life Tips](../student-life/) for more.
- **Delivery** - Community food runners deliver within campus; see the
  [Foodrunners](https://t.me/foodrunneruia) Telegram channel.
- **Water** - Use the water dispensers in your mahallah instead of buying
  drinks.
