Software IIUM provides to students, and other tools students rely on.

## 🏢 From IIUM

- **[Microsoft 365](https://istack.iium.edu.my/books/faq-ict-services-help-desk/page/microsoft-office-for-free)** -
  Free Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint and more) on up to five
  devices. First activate your IIUM Live email (`@live.iium.edu.my`): in
  [i-Ma'luum](https://imaluum.iium.edu.my/), under _My Services_, choose _Office
  365 - Self Register_. Then sign in at
  [portal.office.com](https://portal.office.com/) with that address.
- **[i-Ta'leem](https://italeem.iium.edu.my/)** - The Moodle learning platform
  for your courses. The free [Moodle app](https://download.moodle.org/mobile/)
  works with it on your phone.
- **Library databases** - Journals, e-books and databases from the
  [Dar al-Hikmah Library](https://division.iium.edu.my/lib/subscribed-online-databases-and-ebooks),
  available off campus when you log in with your i-Ma'luum account.

## 📚 Academic & Research

- **[Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/)** and
  **[Mendeley](https://www.mendeley.com/)** - Free reference managers for
  citations and bibliographies.
- **Turnitin** - Similarity checking for assignments and theses, usually set up
  by your lecturer in i-Ta'leem or arranged by your supervisor.
- **Statistics and analysis software** (such as SPSS or NVivo) - Ask your
  kulliyyah's computer lab or your supervisor what is installed and licensed.
