> Finding a place to rent near campus, and avoiding common problems.

IIUM does not guarantee a mahallah room for every student: rooms are offered by
vacancy, first come first served, so many seniors and postgraduate students rent
outside. Apply for a mahallah through
[RSD](https://division.iium.edu.my/rsd/student-residential-mahallah/) first, and
plan off-campus housing if you might not get one.

## 📍 Where Students Rent (Gombak)

- **Taman Melati** - Next to Taman Melati LRT station (Kelana Jaya Line), one
  stop from Gombak station, so it's easy to get to campus and to Kuala Lumpur.
- **Gombak** - The area around the campus and Gombak LRT station is quieter and
  often cheaper, but some places are hilly and slower to reach than they look on
  a map.

Before committing, check the real travel time to your kulliyyah at the time of
your classes, by bus or on foot.

## 🔎 Finding a Place

- **Seniors and community groups** - Rooms are often passed on between students.
  Ask in your kulliyyah's and campus's groups; see
  [Telegram](../social-media/telegramgroups/) and
  [Facebook](../social-media/facebookgroups/) groups.
- **Rental websites** - Search by area name and filter by distance to IIUM.

## ✅ Before You Pay

- **See the place in person**, or have someone you trust visit, before paying
  any deposit.
- **Get a written tenancy agreement** that lists the rent, deposit, utilities
  and notice period, and keep receipts for every payment.
- **Never transfer money to someone you haven't met** for a room you haven't
  seen; rental scams target students.
- **Check safety**: lighting, locks, and how you'll get home after late classes.
  See [Safety & Emergencies](../../student-services/safety/).
