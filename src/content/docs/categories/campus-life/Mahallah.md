Student residences at IIUM are called **mahallah** (plural _mahallat_). They are
run by the
[Residential and Services Department (RSD)](https://division.iium.edu.my/rsd/)
together with the Student Affairs and Development Division (STADD). Room
applications, fees and rules change between sessions, so check the official
pages below before you apply.

## 🔗 Official Links

- [Mahallah portal](https://mahallah.iium.edu.my/) - Each mahallah's own page,
  residential rules and the Council of Principals.
- [RSD: Student Residential](https://division.iium.edu.my/rsd/student-residential-mahallah/) -
  About the mahallah system and how rooms are allocated.
- [RSD Forms](https://division.iium.edu.my/rsd/forms/) - Forms for accommodation
  and other residential matters.
- [i-Ma'luum](https://imaluum.iium.edu.my/) - Apply for and check your room
  through MyMahallah.
- [Fee schedules](../../fees-and-financial-aid/) - Mahallah fees are charged
  with your semester fees.

## 🌴 Other Campuses

Kuantan, Pagoh and the Centre for Foundation Studies in Gambang have their own
mahallat. The [Centre for Foundation Studies](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cfs/)
lists its mahallat in Gambang and Kuantan, and the
[mahallah portal](https://mahallah.iium.edu.my/) covers the rest.
