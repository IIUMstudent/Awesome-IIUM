## 🛂 Student Pass

Your student pass (visa) is processed by **Education Malaysia Global Services
(EMGS)**, not by IIUM directly. In short:

1. Get your offer letter from IIUM.
2. Apply for your student pass through EMGS **from outside Malaysia**, with a
   passport that is valid well beyond your study period. IIUM recommends
   applying at least six weeks before you enrol.
3. When the visa approval letter (e-VAL) is issued, enter Malaysia within the
   time it allows.
4. After you arrive: register at IIUM, do your post-arrival medical check-up,
   and submit your passport to the **IIUM Visa Unit** so the pass can be
   endorsed.

The exact steps, documents and fees are on the
[Office of International Affairs](https://office.iium.edu.my/international/new-application-im14u/)
page and the [EMGS portal](https://visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my/). Renew your
pass before it expires: the Visa Unit can tell you when to start.

## 🏥 Insurance & Medical Check-up

- International students must have **medical insurance (takaful)** for as long
  as they hold a student pass. IIUM provides a compulsory group plan.
- Your **post-arrival medical check-up**, including a chest X-ray, is done
  through the [IIUM Sejahtera Clinic](https://centre.iium.edu.my/clinic/) or an
  EMGS panel clinic. See
  [Health & Wellbeing](../student-services/health-and-wellbeing/).
