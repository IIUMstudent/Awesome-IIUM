> IIUM is spread over several campuses in three states. Here's what is where.

See them all on the [interactive campus map](../../tools/).

## 🏛 Gombak (Selangor) - Main Campus

The main campus and university headquarters, just outside Kuala Lumpur.

- **Kulliyyahs**: [AIKOL](../academics/aikol/), [KAED](../academics/kaed/),
  [KENMS](../academics/kenms/), [KICT](../academics/kict/),
  [KIRKHS](../academics/kirkhs/), [KOE](../academics/koe/) and
  [KOED](../academics/koed/).
- **Housing**: 17 [mahallat](../campus-life/mahallah/).
- **Getting there**: RapidKL bus T200 connects the campus to Gombak LRT station
  (Kelana Jaya Line). See [Campus Life](../campus-life/) for transport tips.
- **Landmarks**: Sultan Haji Ahmad Shah Mosque,
  [Dar al-Hikmah Library](https://division.iium.edu.my/lib/) and the
  [IIUM Sejahtera Clinic](https://centre.iium.edu.my/clinic/).

## 🏥 Kuantan (Pahang)

At Bandar Indera Mahkota, Kuantan: IIUM's health sciences campus.

- **Kulliyyahs**: [KAHS](../academics/kahs/), [KOD](../academics/kod/),
  [KOM](../academics/kom/), [KON](../academics/kon/), [KOP](../academics/kop/)
  and [KOS](../academics/kos/).
- **Teaching hospital**:
  [Sultan Ahmad Shah Medical Centre @IIUM (SASMEC)](https://sasmec.iium.edu.my/),
  where the health sciences kulliyyahs train.

## 🌴 Pagoh (Johor)

Part of the Pagoh Higher Education Hub in Muar, Johor.

- **Kulliyyah**: [KSTCL](../academics/kstcl/), the Kulliyyah of Sustainable
  Tourism and Contemporary Languages (formerly KLM).
- **Course registration**: Pagoh's dates can differ; see
  [Course Registration (Pagoh)](../academics/pre-reg-pagoh/).

## 📘 Gambang (Pahang) - Foundation Studies

Home of the
[Centre for Foundation Studies (CFS)](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cfs/), which
prepares students, mainly SPM holders, for IIUM degree programmes. CFS also has
students at the Kuantan campus.
