The Kulliyyah of Economics and Management Sciences (KENMS) was one of IIUM's
first two faculties, founded in 1983. It teaches economics, business, accounting
and finance, including Islamic banking and finance, at the Gombak campus.

## 🏛️ Official Links

- [KENMS Website](https://kulliyyah.iium.edu.my/kenms/) - Programmes, staff and
  the current management team.
- [About KENMS](https://kulliyyah.iium.edu.my/kenms/about-kenms/) - History,
  vision and mission.
- [Minor Packages & Free Electives](https://iium.edu.my/programme/show/minor-packages-and-free-elective-courses) -
  Minors open to students from every kulliyyah, such as Accounting,
  Entrepreneurship, Islamic Economics and Islamic Finance.

## 📚 Departments

- Accounting
- Business Administration
- Economics
- Finance

## 📖 Academic Programmes

Undergraduate degrees in accounting, business administration, economics and
finance, plus master's and PhD programmes. Current programmes are on the
[KENMS website](https://kulliyyah.iium.edu.my/kenms/).
