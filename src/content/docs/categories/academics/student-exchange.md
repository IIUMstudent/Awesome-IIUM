> Spend a semester, a summer or a few weeks at a university abroad.

Outbound mobility is handled by the
[Office of International Affairs](https://office.iium.edu.my/international/),
together with your kulliyyah.

## 🏛 Official Links

- [Mobility FAQ](https://office.iium.edu.my/international/mobility/faqs) -
  Eligibility, how to apply, documents and each semester's application deadline.
- [Office of International Affairs](https://office.iium.edu.my/international/) -
  Partner universities and international programmes.

## ✈️ Types of Programme

- **Student exchange** - Study at a partner university for one semester, up to
  one year, while staying registered at IIUM. Undergraduates take a full course
  load there that can count towards their IIUM degree.
- **Short programmes** - Outreach programmes of one to two weeks during semester
  breaks, and summer programmes, usually in small groups and at your own cost.
- **Research, internship and visiting programmes** abroad.
- **Scholarship-funded exchanges** such as Erasmus+, Mevlana and Global UGRAD,
  when calls are open.

## 📝 How to Apply

1. **Talk to your kulliyyah first**, so the courses you take abroad will count
   towards your degree.
2. **Check the deadline** for the semester you want in the
   [Mobility FAQ](https://office.iium.edu.my/international/mobility/faqs).
   Applications close months before the semester starts.
3. **Fill in the IIUM outbound application form** and send it with the documents
   on the checklist, as the FAQ explains. Incomplete applications are not
   processed.
4. **Plan your money**: you usually pay your own travel and living costs, and
   you may still owe IIUM fees. See
   [Fees & Financial Aid](../../fees-and-financial-aid/) for funding.
