> What the university expects of you academically, and where the official rules
> are written down.

IIUM's undergraduate rules are set by the Senate in the **Student Academic
Performance Evaluation (Undergraduate) Regulations**, known as **SAPER**. The
rules change from time to time, so read the current version rather than relying
on what seniors tell you.

## 📜 Official Documents

- [SAPER 2021](https://division.iium.edu.my/amad/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2023/11/SAPER-2021-as-of-24-Feb2023-496th-Senate-Meeting.pdf) -
  The undergraduate regulations, as amended up to the 496th Senate meeting
  (February 2023). Check [AMAD](https://division.iium.edu.my/amad/) for a newer
  version.
- [Admission Regulations (Undergraduate)](https://division.iium.edu.my/amad/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2024/05/Admission-Regulations-as-of15-for-DCM-April-2015.pdf) -
  The rules for admission and entry requirements.
- [CPS Procedures](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cps/cps_sop/) - Postgraduate
  students follow the Centre for Postgraduate Studies' regulations and
  procedures instead.

## 📖 What SAPER Covers

- **Course registration** - registering, adding and dropping courses,
  withdrawing from a course, taking a **leave of absence** (deferring a
  semester) and withdrawing from the university.
- **Course evaluation** - how courses are assessed and graded, and how your GPA
  and CGPA are calculated. Try the [GPA calculator](../../../tools/).
- **Academic standing** - what counts as good standing, when you are put on
  **academic probation**, when you can be **dismissed**, and how to apply for
  **readmission**.

## 🧭 Common Situations

| You want to…                  | Start here                                                                                                        |
| :---------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Defer a semester              | Your kulliyyah's academic office, then AMAD. See _leave of absence_ in SAPER.                                     |
| Change programme or kulliyyah | [AMAD](https://division.iium.edu.my/amad/) publishes the application period in each semester's _Important Dates_. |
| Appeal a grade or a dismissal | Your kulliyyah's academic office. Deadlines are short, so act as soon as results are released.                    |
| Check registration deadlines  | [Course Registration](../pre-reg-gombak-kuantan/)                                                                 |
| Check if you can graduate     | [Graduation & Convocation](../graduation/)                                                                        |

If your results are slipping, talk to your academic advisor early, and to the
[Counselling and Career Services Centre](https://centre.iium.edu.my/ccsc/) if
stress or personal problems are part of it.
