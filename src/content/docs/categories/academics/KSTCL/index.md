The Kulliyyah of Sustainable Tourism and Contemporary Languages (KSTCL) is
IIUM's kulliyyah at the Pagoh campus in Johor. It was known as the Kulliyyah of
Languages and Management (KLM) until 2024, when the rebranding was approved by
the Ministry of Higher Education and the IIUM Senate. It teaches tourism with a
focus on sustainability, and languages including Malay, English and Arabic.

Older documents, forms and social media posts may still say **KLM**: they mean
this kulliyyah.
