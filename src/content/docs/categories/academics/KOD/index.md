The Kulliyyah of Dentistry (KOD) trains dentists at IIUM's Kuantan campus, in
its own dental complex at Bandar Indera Mahkota.

## 🏛️ Official Links

- [KOD Website](https://kulliyyah.iium.edu.my/kod/) - Programmes, staff, the
  current management team and events.

## 📚 Departments

- Fundamental Dental and Medical Sciences
- Oral Maxillofacial Surgery and Oral Diagnosis
- Orthodontics
- Paediatric Dentistry and Dental Public Health
- Prosthodontics
- Restorative Dentistry

## 📖 Academic Programmes

- **Undergraduate**: Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS).
- **Postgraduate**: master's, clinical specialist and PhD programmes; see the
  [KOD website](https://kulliyyah.iium.edu.my/kod/).

KOD follows its own academic calendar, separate from most other kulliyyahs.
Check the KOD website and [AMAD](https://division.iium.edu.my/amad/) for dates.
