Essential academic tools and resources for all IIUM students.

## 🏛 University Portals <VerifiedBadge variant="official" text="Official" />

- [i-Ma'luum](https://imaluum.iium.edu.my/) - Access course registration, exam
  results, and generate your timetable.
- [i-Ta'leem](https://italeem.iium.edu.my/) - Learning Management System for
  course materials, assignments, and quizzes.
- [Academic Management and Admission Division (AMAD)](https://division.iium.edu.my/amad/) -
  Academic calendars, _Important Dates_ for each semester, and exam timetables.
- [Course Schedule (my.iium)](https://my.iium.edu.my/) - Check offered courses
  and class schedules before registration.

## 📚 Library & Research <VerifiedBadge variant="verified" />

- [Dar al-Hikmah Library](https://division.iium.edu.my/lib/) - Main library
  website with all services and resources.
- [Online Databases & E-books](https://division.iium.edu.my/lib/subscribed-online-databases-and-ebooks) -
  Journals and databases IIUM subscribes to. Log in with your i-Ma'luum account
  to use them off campus.
- [IIUM Repository (IREP)](http://irep.iium.edu.my/) - Access to research
  papers, theses, and academic publications.
- [EZProxy Access](https://ezproxy.iium.edu.my/) - Off-campus access to
  subscribed databases and journals.

## 🛠 Academic Tools

- [IIUM Schedule Maker](https://github.com/iiumschedule/iium-schedule-maker) -
  Visualize and plan your semester timetable before registration.
- [CGPA Calculator](https://github.com/zamzameir/iiumcgpa) - Calculate your GPA
  and CGPA easily with IIUM's grading scale.
- [Thesis LaTeX Template](https://github.com/Ayman-Elfaki/IIUM-Latex-Template) -
  Official LaTeX templates for IIUM theses and dissertations.
