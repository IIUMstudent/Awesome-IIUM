## 🎓 How It Works

1. **Complete your programme** and clear any outstanding fees; the graduation
   fee is charged with your semester fees.
2. **Check the name list** on the
   [convocation website](https://convo.iium.edu.my/) once it is published. Your
   invitation is sent to the email address registered in i-Ma'luum, so keep it
   up to date.
3. **Collect your robe** using the forms on the convocation website, and read
   the FAQ for the dress code and what to bring on the day.
4. **Attend convocation**, usually held once a year at the Gombak campus.
