> Preparing for life after graduation.

## 🏛 Official Career Services

- [Counselling and Career Services Centre (CCSC)](https://centre.iium.edu.my/ccsc/) -
  Career consultations, CV reviews, career readiness programmes and the IIUM
  Career Fair. Offices at Gombak, Kuantan, Pagoh and Gambang
  ([contacts](https://centre.iium.edu.my/ccsc/contact-us/)).
- [Entrepreneurship Development Centre (EDC)](https://centre.iium.edu.my/edc) -
  Support for students who want to start a business.
- [Centre for Professional Development (CPD)](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cpd) -
  Professional courses and certificates.

## 💼 Internships (Industrial Training)

Internships are arranged through your kulliyyah: each programme sets its own
industrial training rules, timing and forms. Start with your kulliyyah's page in
[Academics](../academics/) and its industrial training or student development
office.

- **CCSC vacancy listings**: check the
  [CCSC website](https://centre.iium.edu.my/ccsc/) for current openings.

## 🔎 Job Search

- [Career for IIUM Graduates](https://t.me/s/carikerjauia) - Telegram channel
  with job openings shared by the community.
- [IIUM on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/school/iium/) - Find alumni at
  companies you are interested in.
- [HURIS](https://huris.iium.edu.my/) - Job openings at IIUM itself.
