> Master's and PhD study at IIUM.

Postgraduate admission and records are handled by the **Centre for Postgraduate
Studies (CPS)**, and your programme is run by your kulliyyah.

## 🏛 Official Links

- [Centre for Postgraduate Studies (CPS)](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cps/) -
  Programmes, admissions, forms and announcements for postgraduate students.
- [e-Admission](https://eadmission.iium.edu.my/) - Apply online. Register an
  account with your email address first.
- [General Admission Requirements](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cps/general-admission-requirements/) -
  Academic and language requirements.
- [CPS FAQ](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cps/faq) - Intakes, closing dates and
  common questions.
- [CPS Procedures (SOP)](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cps/cps_sop/) - Admission,
  records, academic progress, viva voce and graduation procedures.
- [CPS Offices & Contacts](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cps/offices-and-services) -
  Who to contact at CPS.

## 📝 Applying

Applications are open all year. Each intake has a closing date, which is earlier
for international applicants; if you miss it, your application moves to the next
intake. The current dates are in the
[CPS FAQ](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cps/faq).

## 🔬 Research Support

- [Dar al-Hikmah Library](https://division.iium.edu.my/lib/) - Databases and
