> Looking after your body and mind while you study.

## 🏥 Clinic & Hospital

- [IIUM Sejahtera Clinic (ISC)](https://centre.iium.edu.my/clinic/) - The
  university clinic at Gombak, for students and staff. It also runs the
  **medical check-up** that new students must complete; book it after your
  student status is active.
- [Sultan Ahmad Shah Medical Centre @IIUM (SASMEC)](https://sasmec.iium.edu.my/) -
  IIUM's teaching hospital in Kuantan.

## 🛡 Insurance

IIUM students are covered by a group medical insurance (takaful) scheme while
enrolled; international students must keep compulsory medical insurance for
their student pass. Check what your plan covers, and how to get a guarantee
letter for hospital admission, with the clinic or the
[Office of International Affairs](https://office.iium.edu.my/international/).

## 💬 Counselling & Mental Health

- [Counselling and Career Services Centre (CCSC)](https://centre.iium.edu.my/ccsc/) -
  Confidential individual and group counselling for students, with offices at
  Gombak, Kuantan, Pagoh and Gambang
  ([contacts](https://centre.iium.edu.my/ccsc/contact-us/)).
- [Befrienders Kuala Lumpur](https://www.befrienders.org.my/) - Free,
  confidential emotional support any time of day, outside IIUM.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call **999**.

## ♿ Disability Support

- [Disability Services Unit (DSU)](https://office.iium.edu.my/dsu) - Support and
  accommodations for students with disabilities.
