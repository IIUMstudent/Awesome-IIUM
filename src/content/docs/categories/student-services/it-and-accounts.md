> Your IIUM accounts and how to fix them when they stop working.

## 🔑 Your Accounts

- **i-Ma'luum** ([imaluum.iium.edu.my](https://imaluum.iium.edu.my/)) - Your
  main student account. Your matric number and i-Ma'luum password also log you
  in to campus Wi-Fi and the library's online databases.
- **i-Ta'leem** ([italeem.iium.edu.my](https://italeem.iium.edu.my/)) - The
  learning management system for your courses.
- **IIUM Live email** (`@live.iium.edu.my`) - Your Microsoft 365 account, with
  free Office. Activate it in i-Ma'luum under _My Services_ → _Office 365 - Self
  Register_; see [Essential Software](../../campus-life/software/).
- **Forgot your password?** Reset it at
  [passwordreset.iium.edu.my](https://passwordreset.iium.edu.my/), or contact
  the ICT Help Desk if your account is locked.

## 📶 Wi-Fi

Connect to the **IIUM-Student** network, then sign in on the login page that
opens with your matric number and your i-Ma'luum password. Step-by-step guides
and fixes for common problems are in the
[ICT Help Desk FAQ](https://istack.iium.edu.my/books/faq-ict-services-help-desk/page/how-to-connect-to-iium-student-wi-fi).

## 🆘 ICT Help Desk

The Information Technology Division (ITD) runs a single help desk for students
and staff: account problems, Wi-Fi, email and other IT issues.

- [ICT Help Desk FAQ](https://istack.iium.edu.my/books/faq-ict-services-help-desk) -
  Answers to common problems.
- [Service Desk](http://servicedesk.iium.edu.my/) - Report a problem.
- Email: `servicedesk@iium.edu.my`
