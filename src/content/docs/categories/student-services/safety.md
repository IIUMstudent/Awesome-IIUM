> Who to call when something goes wrong.

## 🚨 In an Emergency

- **Police, ambulance and fire (Malaysia): 999**, or **112** from a mobile
  phone.
- **IIUM security (Gombak): 03-6421 5555**. The
  [Office of Security Management (OSeM)](https://office.iium.edu.my/osem/) lists
  the current numbers for every campus, including its 24-hour operations room.

Save your campus's security number in your phone before you need it.

## 🛡 Campus Security

The [Office of Security Management (OSeM)](https://office.iium.edu.my/osem/)
runs the **IIUM Auxiliary Police**, who patrol the campuses. Contact them about
accidents, theft, lost and found items, suspicious activity and vehicle matters.

## 🚶 Staying Safe

- Use the official IIUM bus services or registered transport. For sisters, the
  community runs a
  [sisters-only transporter group](https://t.me/+bYgcuJuMLjgxMTk1).
- Follow your mahallah's rules on visitors and curfews.
- During holidays, watch for OSeM and IIUM announcements about which gates are
  open.
