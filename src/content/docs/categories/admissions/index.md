> Where to apply, what you need, and what happens after you get an offer.

Entry requirements, intakes and application fees change, so this page explains
the routes and links to the official pages that publish the current details.

## 🧭 Choose Your Route

| You are…                                   | Apply through                                                                                                       |
| :----------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Malaysian, after SPM/STPM/matriculation    | [UPU Online](https://upu.mohe.gov.my/) (public university admissions) or directly to IIUM                           |
| Malaysian, after SPM, wanting a foundation | The [Centre for Foundation Studies (CFS)](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cfs/), which leads into IIUM degree programmes |
| International (undergraduate)              | Directly to IIUM; see [International Students](../international-students/)                                          |
| Postgraduate (master's or PhD)             | [e-Admission](https://eadmission.iium.edu.my/); see [Postgraduate Studies](../postgraduate/)                        |

## 🏛 Official Links

- [Admission Information](https://iium.edu.my/my/page/admission-information) -
  General entry requirements, including the English and Arabic language
  requirements.
- [Academic Management and Admission Division (AMAD)](https://division.iium.edu.my/amad/) -
  The division that handles admissions, registration and academic records.
- [Centre for Foundation Studies](https://centre.iium.edu.my/cfs/) - Foundation
  programmes at Gambang, mainly for SPM holders.
- [UPU Online](https://upu.mohe.gov.my/) - The Ministry of Higher Education's
  application system for Malaysian students.

## 🗣 Language Requirements

Most programmes are taught in English, and some in Arabic. If you don't have an
accepted English test result (such as IELTS or TOEFL), IIUM tests you with its
own placement test when you arrive, and you may need to take language courses
before your degree courses. The required scores are on the
[Admission Information](https://iium.edu.my/my/page/admission-information) page.

## ✅ After You Get an Offer

1. **Accept online** before the deadline in your offer letter, or the offer is
   withdrawn.
2. **Pay your fees** and check what you owe: see
   [Fees & Financial Aid](../fees-and-financial-aid/).
3. **Apply for a mahallah** (on-campus housing): see
   [Mahallahs](../campus-life/mahallah/).
4. **Book your medical check-up**: new students must do one at the
   [IIUM Sejahtera Clinic](https://centre.iium.edu.my/clinic/) (see
   [Health & Wellbeing](../student-services/health-and-wellbeing/)).
5. **Attend Ta'aruf** (orientation week) and register your courses: see
   [Course Registration](../academics/pre-reg-gombak-kuantan/).
