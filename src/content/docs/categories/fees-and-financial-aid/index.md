> What you pay, how to pay it, and where to get help paying it.

Fees depend on your programme, your intake year and whether you are Malaysian or
international, and IIUM revises them from time to time. Always use the latest
fee schedule from the Finance Division rather than figures you see elsewhere.

## 💳 Fees

- [Finance Division](https://division.iium.edu.my/finance/) - Publishes the fee
  schedules (as PDFs) for local and international, undergraduate and
  postgraduate students.
- [EzPAY](https://ezpay.iium.edu.my/) - Pay your fees online.
- [i-Ma'luum](https://imaluum.iium.edu.my/) - Check your statement and what you
  owe.

Semester fees usually include tuition and recurring fees, plus mahallah (room)
fees if you live on campus. A one-off entrance fee is charged when you join and
a graduation fee when you finish.

## 🎓 Scholarships & Financial Aid from IIUM

- [IIUM Endowment Fund (IEF)](https://division.iium.edu.my/ief) - IIUM's main
  source of student aid, funded by donations, zakat and waqf. It runs the
  **Ummatic Scholarship**, financial assistance, and an **emergency fund** for
  students in sudden difficulty.
- [IEF Forms](https://division.iium.edu.my/ief/downloadable-forms) - Application
  forms for IEF assistance, including zakat.
- [Finance Division: Scholarships](https://division.iium.edu.my/finance/scholarship/) -
  Other scholarships and fee reductions managed by IIUM.

### Zakat

IEF is a collection agent (sub-amil) for
[Lembaga Zakat Selangor](https://www.zakatselangor.com.my/), and a share of the
zakat collected through IIUM goes back to helping eligible IIUM students. Apply
through IEF.

## 🇲🇾 Loans & Scholarships from Outside IIUM

- [PTPTN](https://www.ptptn.gov.my/) - The national education loan for Malaysian
  students.
- [MARA](https://www.mara.gov.my/) - Education sponsorship for eligible
  Bumiputera students.
- [Biasiswa Malaysia](https://t.me/BiasiswaMalaysia) - Telegram channel that
  shares scholarship openings.

## 🆘 In Financial Difficulty?

Talk to the IEF office about the emergency fund, and to your kulliyyah's student
development office. The
[Counselling and Career Services Centre](https://centre.iium.edu.my/ccsc/) can
help if money worries are affecting your studies.
