⚖️ Ahmad Ibrahim Kulliyyah of Laws (AIKOL)

The Ahmad Ibrahim Kulliyyah of Laws (AIKOL) at the Gombak campus teaches both civil law and Shariah. It is named after the late Emeritus Professor Tan Sri Ahmad bin Mohamed Ibrahim, its founding father.

📖 Academic Programmes

Section titled “📖 Academic Programmes”
  • Undergraduate: Bachelor of Laws (LL.B) (Honours) and Bachelor of Laws with Shariah (LL.B Shariah) (Honours).
  • Postgraduate: master’s and PhD programmes in law, plus the Diploma in Syariah Legal Practice (DSLP) and the Diploma in Administration of Islamic Judiciary (DAIJ), professional qualifications for Shariah lawyers, judges and officers.

Current programme structures are on the AIKOL website.
